## English

AIChats is an open-source Chrome extension that lets you switch between and
manage multiple AI chat websites from the side panel.

Features:
- Side panel — open AI chat websites in Chrome's side panel and switch with one
  click, no more tab juggling
- Smart Quick Prompts — template prompts are filled into the chat input
  automatically, with optional one-tap submit
- Right-click menu — select text and right-click to send it, translate it, or
  run a quick prompt
- Keep-alive sessions — switching chats never loses your conversations or drafts
- Custom sites — add any AI chat website, or pick from 10 pre-configured templates
- Themes & languages — dark, light or system theme; English and Simplified Chinese

Open source: https://github.com/syeplz/AIChats
